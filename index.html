Jarvis Personal Assistant

Jarvis is a privately operated AI assistant designed to help its owner organise information and manage personal tasks.

Google account access

With the owner's authorisation, Jarvis can access Gmail and Google Calendar to support personal productivity and information retrieval.

Google account access is intended for the owner's personal use. Access is limited to the permissions granted during Google's authorisation process.
