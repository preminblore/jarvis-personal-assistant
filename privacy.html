Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Overview

Jarvis Personal Assistant is a privately operated AI assistant intended for the personal productivity of its owner. This policy explains how it handles Google account data accessed with the owner's permission.

Google data accessed

With the owner's authorisation, Jarvis may access Gmail messages and Google Calendar events within the permissions granted through Google's authorisation process. The current integration is configured for read-only access.

Purpose of access

Google data is accessed to help the owner search and understand personal email, review calendar events, and prepare personal productivity summaries.

Storage and processing

Google account data may be processed by the assistant and its configured AI service to fulfil the owner's requests. Authentication credentials and tokens are stored on the owner's private server. Data handling depends on the configured services and their respective policies. The owner should avoid using the assistant to process information they do not want sent to those services.

Sharing

Google account data is not intended to be sold or shared for advertising. Data may be processed by service providers required to operate the assistant, according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Data may remain in local application storage, logs, or backups where applicable. The owner can remove stored data and credentials through the relevant application or service configuration.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke Jarvis's Google account access through their Google Account's third-party connections settings.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the owner of this privately operated application.
